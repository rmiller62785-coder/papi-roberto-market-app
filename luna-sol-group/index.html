Where it was

A demand shock overwhelmed the existing operating system.

A May 14 pricing update was followed by an approximately 20% submission spike, adding roughly 1.6 million cards to the queue within days. PSA announced a pause of its four lowest-cost Value tiers effective June 2. The publicly reported queue, initially described as nearly 10 million cards, was later revised upward to a record approximately 14 million.

Where it is now

Public reporting showed a meaningful operating recovery by mid-July.

Independent reporting through mid-July put the backlog at roughly 11 to 12 million cards, down from the record ~14 million June peak — the exact figure varies slightly by outlet and reporting date, but the trajectory is consistent. June grading increased about 10% over May, approximately 2.5 million cards were graded in June, and the first week of July produced roughly 492,900 graded items while maintaining a 99.4% operational success rate.
