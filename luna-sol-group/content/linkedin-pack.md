## 4 — Free Backlog Recovery Calculator

**Hook**
I generalized the model behind the PSA case study into a free tool. If you run any kind of queue —
tickets, claims, orders, inspections — you can model your own recovery in about two minutes.

**Body**
Ten inputs: current backlog, target threshold, weekly inbound, rated capacity, utilization, rework,
aging-work mix, surge capacity, planned productivity gain, and target window.

Four outputs: net backlog burn, weeks to threshold, the capacity gap between what you have and what
you need, and a confidence range.

Nothing is stored unless you choose to send me the scenario. No signup wall.
